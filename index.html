Privacy Policy · Effective 31 October 2026

FoodVibe helps you find people selling homemade and street food near you — and helps small sellers be found. FoodVibe is a product of VFX Cook Entertainment Pvt Ltd, Bengaluru, India (“we”, “us”).

This policy explains, in plain words, what information the FoodVibe app collects, why, and what you can do about it. It follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

01. What we collect

  • Your account: your name and email address, from Sign in with Google or from an email sign-in code. Your phone number only if you choose to add it as a contact.
  • If you sell: your display name, the food items you list (photos, names, prices), your shop spot if you are a fixed seller, your opening and closing times, and — only if you choose to add it — your UPI ID and/or a photo of your UPI QR code, so buyers can pay you.
  • Seller location, only while selling: when you turn “Selling now” ON, your current location is shared so buyers nearby can find you and walk to you. Each update replaces the previous one. When you turn selling OFF, your spot is no longer shown.
  • Buyer location: used on your phone to show how far sellers are from you. We do not keep a record of where you have been.
  • Purchases and ratings: what you bought from a seller, whether you said you paid (and how — UPI or cash), and the star rating you gave. We cannot see or verify your actual payment — payments go directly between you and the seller.
  • “I want it tomorrow” requests: the item and quantity you ask a seller for the next day, so the seller can plan.
  • Meet-up signals: when you tap “Walk to them” or use the meet-up blinker, the seller is told someone is coming. This is how the app helps you find each other.
  • Consent and safety records: a record that you agreed to this policy, and any reports you make about a seller or buyer.

02. Why we collect it

  • To show buyers the sellers near them, and help them walk to the seller.
  • To let sellers list food, be found, and receive payment directly.
  • To show ratings, so buyers can trust sellers.
  • To keep the marketplace safe (for example, acting on reports).
  • To obey the law.

We ask for your consent before collecting your data, in the language you choose in the app. You can say no — the app simply cannot show your selling spot or save your lists without it.

03. What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your personal data to anyone.
  • We never store a history of your movements or locations.
  • We do not show advertisements, and we include no advertising or analytics trackers in the app.
  • We do not profile you or make automated decisions about you.
  • Payments never pass through us. Money goes directly from buyer to seller (UPI or cash).

04. Who can see what

  • Other app users can see what you publish as a seller: your display name, your food items and prices, your selling spot while you are selling, your opening hours, your ratings, and your UPI ID/QR only if you chose to add it.
  • Our hosting provider (Supabase) stores the app's data on secure servers in Mumbai, India, and processes it only to run the app for us.
  • Authorities: we may disclose information only if the law requires it.

05. How long we keep it

  • Your account and listings stay while your account exists, so the app can work for you.
  • A seller's live location is replaced by each new update and stops being shown when selling is turned off. We do not build a history from it.
  • When you delete your data (see below), we erase your personal data from our active systems, except where the law requires us to keep a record.

06. Your rights — “My data” in the app

In the app, under Profile → My data, you can at any time:

  • See and download the data we hold about you.
  • Pause location sharing.
  • Delete everything — your account and your data are erased.
  • Withdraw consent — by deleting your data, turning selling off, or writing to us.

You can also correct anything wrong in your profile from within the app, or by writing to us.

07. Children

Sellers on FoodVibe must be 18 years or older, confirmed during sign-up. FoodVibe is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect children's data.

08. Security

Data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections, and access inside our systems is limited to what is needed to run the service. If a data breach ever affects your personal data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.
